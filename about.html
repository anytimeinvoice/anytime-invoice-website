Built by people who got tired of invoicing software built for accountants

Most accounting software is designed for the person doing the books — not the person doing the job. It assumes you're sitting at a desk, not standing in a driveway with dust on your hands and five minutes before the next call.

Anytime Invoice is built for Australian sole traders, tradespeople, builders and freelancers who need to quote a job, send an ATO-compliant tax invoice, and get paid — all from a phone, without a bookkeeping degree.

That means voice-dictated invoices instead of forms, GST handled correctly by default instead of a setting you have to find, and a payment page your client can use without creating an account. It means the free tier is actually usable, not a crippled trial.

The goal is simple: quote on site, get paid before you're off the job, and get your Sunday nights back.
